# Rain (Raincards)

> Rain is a stablecoin payments infrastructure company that lets platforms, fintechs, and institutions issue cards, open virtual accounts and wallets, and move money globally through a single API. Its issuing platform turns self-custodied stablecoin collateral and digital-dollar balances into Visa/Mastercard-accepted spending at 175M+ merchant locations, connecting blockchain settlement to traditional card networks.

This llms.txt was generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the public catalog record; Rain's own OpenAPI/Swagger reference is behind a login-gated Mintlify portal and was not publicly retrievable.

## Products
- Card issuing (corporate / spend cards)
- Virtual accounts and stablecoin wallets
- Onramps and offramps
- Cross-border payment processing and global money movement
